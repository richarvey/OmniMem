# Fonts

The OmniMem design system's two typefaces, as Fontsource's variable Latin
subsets (weights 100 to 900), both under the SIL Open Font License 1.1
(https://openfontlicense.org):

- `onest-variable-latin.woff2`: Onest, by Dmitri Voloshin (@fontsource-variable/onest 5.3.1)
- `geist-mono-variable-latin.woff2`: Geist Mono, by Vercel (@fontsource-variable/geist-mono 5.3.0)
